1. Who we are and what Clerking is

Clerking is a technology and matchmaking platform that connects licensed attorneys with law students for supervised legal support work. Clerking is operated by Rayven-Nikkita Collins LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our").

Clerking is not a law firm and does not provide legal services, legal advice, or legal representation. We do not employ law students for legal work. All student work is performed under the direct supervision of the hiring attorney, pursuant to a separate engagement agreement between the attorney and the student, in compliance with ABA Model Rule 5.3 or your applicable state equivalent.

Fees are billed in advance through Stripe.

For students: Clerking provides resource libraries, job board access, and matching services at tiers described on the site (Resource, Member, Placement). Subscriptions are month-to-month and billed through Stripe.

Clerking is never in the payment chain between attorneys and students. Attorneys pay students directly, pursuant to the engagement agreement between them. Clerking subscription fees cover platform and matchmaking access only.
