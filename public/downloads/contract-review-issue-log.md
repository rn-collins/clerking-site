## Review sequence
1. Confirm document set and version.
2. Inventory defined terms and cross-references.
3. Compare text only against the attorney-approved playbook.
4. Log issues without inventing a fallback or risk score.
5. Separate legal questions from business questions.
6. Route every proposed change to the supervising attorney.
7. Preserve final decision, owner, and version history.
