## Review gates
- Separate event date from document date.
- Preserve contradictory versions; do not silently reconcile them.
- Flag gaps, estimated dates, hearsay, privilege, sealing, and discovery restrictions.
- Link every event to a source and precise location.
- Do not upload client records to an unapproved system.
- Supervising attorney decides legal significance and use.
