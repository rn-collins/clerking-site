Privacy Policy

How Clerking collects, uses, and protects your information.

Effective date: July 1, 2026
Operator: Rayven-Nikkita Collins LLC (DBA Clerking), a Massachusetts limited liability company.
Questions? rn@rncollins.com

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Rayven-Nikkita Collins LLC ("Clerking," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Clerking website and platform (collectively, the "Services"). It applies to attorneys, law students, and any other visitors to the Clerking site.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, email address, and contact information when you sign up, subscribe, or contact us;
  • Professional information (bar license number, jurisdiction, law school, class year) as part of the onboarding or vetting process;
  • Payment information, processed directly by Stripe — Clerking does not store or have access to your full card number or banking details;
  • Communications you send to us by email or through the platform.

Information collected automatically:

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URL, time and date of visits);
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences and improve platform functionality.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the platform and provide the Services you've requested;
  • Process subscriptions and payments through Stripe;
  • Match attorneys with student candidates based on practice area, availability, and preferences;
  • Communicate with you about your account, placements, and platform updates;
  • Verify attorney licensure and student enrollment for platform eligibility;
  • Improve the platform, detect fraud, and maintain security;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Information sharing

We share your information only as follows:

  • Attorney–student matches: When we propose a match, we share relevant profile information (name, school, class year, practice area focus, writing sample for students; name, firm, practice area for attorneys) with the proposed match. You may decline any match before sharing is confirmed.
  • Stripe: Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. Your payment information is subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers: We may share information with third-party vendors who help us operate the Services (e.g., email delivery, analytics), under contracts that restrict their use of your data.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or in response to a valid legal process.
  • Business transfers: If Clerking is acquired or merged, your information may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to the same privacy protections.

5. Client confidentiality and work product

Clerking takes confidentiality seriously. We do not access, review, or store the substantive work product, case materials, or client communications exchanged between attorneys and students in the course of a placement. Those materials are shared directly between the attorney and student pursuant to their engagement agreement, which requires the student to maintain confidentiality under applicable professional responsibility rules.

6. Data retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, we retain your information for a reasonable period to resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and comply with legal obligations, after which we delete or anonymize it. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing rn@rncollins.com.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to operate the platform (session management, authentication). We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. No transmission over the internet is fully secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at rn@rncollins.com.

9. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to our legal retention obligations;
  • Opt out of marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email from us.

To exercise these rights, email rn@rncollins.com. We will respond within 30 days.

11. Third-party links

The Clerking site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Stripe, Outlook booking, Gumroad). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy with a new effective date. Your continued use of the Services after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
